# Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026. This covers Cairn for iPhone and Cairn for Mac.

Cairn has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. The developer
receives nothing from either app and cannot see your books, your reading position or how you use
the app.

## Cairn for iPhone

- **iCloud.** Books arrive from your Mac through your own private iCloud database, and the place
  you stopped in each book is saved back to it so your devices agree. That database belongs to
  your Apple account; Apple stores it under its own privacy policy, and the developer has no
  access to it.
- **On the device.** Downloaded books and your settings (language, autoplay, captions, playback speed) are
  kept on the iPhone and removed when you delete the app.
- **Nothing else.** The app contacts no other service, includes no third-party tracking code and
  asks for no permission to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

## Cairn for Mac

The Mac app makes the books, and to do that it sends data to services **you** choose and
configure. Each one handles what it receives under its own terms.

- **The model provider you select** receives the text of the book you add, in order to write its
  path, slides and narration script.
- **Microsoft's speech service** receives the narration script, in order to return the audio.
- **The search service you select** (Firecrawl, Brave Search or Tavily) receives the queries the
  companion writes when you ask it something beyond the book. A query may contain brief context
  from the book.
- **WeChat Reading**, only if you sign in to it, receives the book's title and author to match
  it; highlights, progress and chapter titles are then fetched for that book.
- **iCloud**, only if you turn syncing on, receives each finished book's path, slides, narration
  audio and cover. The book's own text is never uploaded.

Books, generated material and API keys are stored on your Mac.

## Removing your data

Touch and hold a book on the iPhone shelf to remove it from iCloud and from the phone. Deleting
the app removes everything it stored on the device. Data in your iCloud account can also be
managed in the system's iCloud settings.

## Children

Cairn is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date. Questions:
<https://github.com/jiehaoZ/Cairn/issues>.

# 隐私政策

2026 年 10 月 6 日生效。适用于 iPhone 版和 Mac 版 Cairn。

Cairn 没有账号系统，没有统计分析，没有广告，也没有自己的服务器。开发者不会从这两个应用收到任何数据，
看不到你的书、阅读进度和使用情况。

## iPhone 版

- **iCloud。** 书通过你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库从 Mac 同步过来，每本书读到哪里也会写回去，让几台设备保持
  一致。这个数据库属于你的 Apple 账号，由 Apple 按它自己的隐私政策保存，开发者无法访问。
- **设备本地。** 下载的书和你的设置（语言、自动播放、字幕、播放速度）保存在 iPhone 上，删除应用时一并删除。
- **仅此而已。** 应用不连接其他任何服务，不含第三方追踪代码，也不请求通讯录、照片、位置、相机或麦克风权限。

## Mac 版

书是在 Mac 上生成的。为此，它会把数据发给**你自己**选择并配置的服务，各服务按其自身条款处理收到的内容。

- **你选择的模型服务商**：收到你添加的书的正文，用来生成路径、幻灯片和旁白稿。
- **微软的语音服务**：收到旁白稿，用来返回音频。
- **你选择的搜索服务**（Firecrawl、Brave Search 或 Tavily）：当你问到书外的内容时，收到助手写的搜索词，
  其中可能带有书里的简短上下文。
- **微信读书**（仅在你登录后）：收到书名和作者用于匹配，随后获取这本书的划线、进度和章节标题。
- **iCloud**（仅在你打开同步后）：收到每本已完成的书的路径、幻灯片、旁白音频和封面。书的正文不会上传。

书、生成的内容和 API 密钥都保存在你的 Mac 上。

## 删除数据

在 iPhone 书架上长按一本书，可以把它从 iCloud 和手机上移除。删除应用会清除它在设备上保存的全部内容。
iCloud 账号里的数据也可以在系统的 iCloud 设置中管理。

## 儿童

Cairn 不面向儿童，也不向任何人收集个人信息。

## 变更与联系

政策如有变更，会在本页更新并标注新的生效日期。有问题请到
